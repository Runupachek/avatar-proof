What BTCSIGNAL.AI does

BTCSIGNAL.AI publishes a daily signal card covering exactly three markets: Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), and Solana (SOL). Every card closes with the line: Not financial advice.

Support

Our support desk answers messages Monday to Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Eastern. You can reach the desk any time through the contact form on btcsignal.ai; messages left outside desk hours are answered on the next desk day.
